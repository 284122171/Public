❓ FAQ

  • Is iBible AI free?
    Yes! iBible AI is completely free to use. You can optionally support development through donations.
  • Do I need an account?
    No account needed. Your data stays on your device — we respect your privacy.
  • Which Bible versions are supported?
    Multiple versions including KJV, ASV, CUV, and more, with automatic language matching.
  • How many languages are supported?
    12 languages: English, Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Italian, Vietnamese, and Arabic.
  • Does the AI require internet?
    Yes, the AI assistant requires an internet connection. Bible reading and favorites work offline.
