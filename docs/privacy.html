Privacy Policy

iBible AI

Last updated: April 15, 2025

Introduction

iBible AI ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

Information We Do Not Collect

iBible AI is designed with privacy in mind:

  • We do not require account registration or sign-in
  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties

Data Stored Locally

All your app data — including favorites, reading preferences, and settings — is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.

AI Chat Feature

When you use the AI Bible assistant, your questions are sent to our AI service to generate responses. We do not store your conversation history on our servers. Conversations are kept only on your device.

Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Apple In-App Purchase: For processing optional donations. Payment is handled entirely by Apple.
  • Bible API: For retrieving Scripture content. No personal data is sent with these requests.

Children's Privacy

iBible AI does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app content is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
